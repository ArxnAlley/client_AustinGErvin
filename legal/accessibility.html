Accessibility

Where this stands

This office wants its website to be usable by everyone — including people using a screen reader, a keyboard alone, magnification, or a reduced-motion setting.

The site is built with that in mind. Every control can be reached with a keyboard, headings and landmarks are structured for screen readers, disclosure controls report their own state, and the motion on the page stops for anyone whose system asks it to.

No formal conformance claim is made here. An independent audit against WCAG has not yet been carried out, so this page does not claim a conformance level it has not earned. That audit is planned before launch.
