# Executive Summary

Austin G. Ervin is a Southern Ohio attorney whose website should
emphasize trust, professionalism, and clarity while avoiding generic
law-firm design patterns.

The website should establish authority immediately, clearly communicate
practice areas, build confidence through trust signals, and convert
visitors into consultations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Goals

-   Build trust immediately.
-   Increase consultation requests.
-   Clearly explain practice areas.
-   Present Austin as an experienced professional.
-   Create a premium online presence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Target Audience

Potential clients searching for legal representation in Southern Ohio.

Visitors are often: - stressed - uncertain - looking for trustworthy
guidance - comparing multiple attorneys

The website should reduce anxiety instead of increasing it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brand Position

Professional.

Honest.

Prepared.

Accessible.

Confident without appearing arrogant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Messaging

Visitors should quickly understand:

-   Who Austin is.
-   What he practices.
-   Who he helps.
-   Why they should trust him.
-   How to contact him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Website Objectives

The website should:

-   build credibility
-   increase local SEO visibility
-   improve conversion
-   communicate expertise
-   become the firm's digital foundation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Trust Signals

Prioritize:

-   attorney biography
-   office photography
-   client reviews
-   awards
-   memberships
-   bar admissions
-   consultation CTA
-   clear contact information

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# SEO Direction

Focus on:

-   individual practice area pages
-   location-based pages
-   FAQ content
-   structured headings
-   internal linking
-   local optimization

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Strategy

Create dedicated pages for:

-   Home
-   About
-   Practice Areas
-   Individual Practice Area pages
-   FAQ
-   Contact

Avoid thin content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Photography

Prefer authentic imagery:

-   Austin
-   Office
-   Courthouse
-   Local community

Avoid obvious stock photography.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# User Experience

Visitors should feel:

-   safe
-   informed
-   confident
-   respected

Navigation should remain simple and predictable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Technical Expectations

-   Responsive
-   Accessible
-   Fast
-   SEO-friendly
-   Reusable component architecture

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Open Questions

Still confirm with Austin:

-   Long-term firm vision
-   Future hiring plans
-   Desired practice growth
-   Preferred case mix
-   Future office expansion
-   Additional trust assets
-   Photography availability
